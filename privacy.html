Privacy & cookies

This page explains how the Chambers Ireland Business Sustainability Action Planner remembers your work and measures visits. It applies to this planner, not to every page on the main Chambers Ireland website.

No account required

Saving your action plan on this device

The planner uses browser local storage to remember your organisation name (if entered), the actions you select, their statuses, and any owners, target dates or notes you add. This is what allows your work to reappear if you return using the same browser and device.

Your checklist and action-plan entries are not automatically uploaded to a Chambers Ireland database. Exporting a PDF creates a file for you to keep; it does not send your selected actions to us.

This browser storage remains until you or your browser clears the site's data. The Clear selections button resets action statuses, but intentionally keeps your saved organisation name, owners, dates and notes. To remove all saved planner information from that device, clear the site data for sdg-simple-model.vercel.app in your browser settings.

Anonymous website statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand general use of this website, such as page views, approximate visitor location, browser or device type and traffic sources. Vercel describes this service as cookie-free, using aggregated statistics rather than accounts or personally identifiable visitor profiles. We cannot use those reports to see the business names or action plans of visitors.

See Vercel's Web Analytics privacy information for details of the data the service processes.

Cookies and the notice you see

We have not added advertising cookies or analytics cookies to this planner. The planner uses local storage for its requested functionality, which is a browser technology covered by many cookie and storage notices. We also use local storage to remember if you have dismissed the privacy notice, so it does not appear on every visit. Both sets of stored information remain until you clear this site's data in your browser.

The notice's Got it button simply dismisses the information message; it is not an agreement to advertising or tracking cookies.

Hosting and other website resources

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with other websites, technical information about requests, such as IP address and browser details, may be processed to deliver and secure the website. The planner also loads Google Fonts and SDG icon images from external services, which can receive technical information associated with requests for those resources.

There is no registration form or login for this planner. If you choose to email Chambers Ireland using a contact link, your message will be handled separately from the planner.

Questions?

Chambers Ireland operates this planner. For questions about the website or its privacy practices, contact info@chambers.ie.

Last updated: 14 October 2026. If we introduce new tracking technologies or change how data is collected, this notice will need updating.
